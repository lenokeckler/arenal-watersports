# State photos

## Intent

Find a machine and see how complete its state photos are (US-OPE-16).

## Requirements

- Only machines of categories that keep condition photos, without retired ones.
- Each card shows the photo of its category, the same one as the board, how many of the four basic photos exist, and the hits.
- On a wide screen the cards grow when a list without pages holds three or fewer.
- Twelve machines per page, filtered by category.
