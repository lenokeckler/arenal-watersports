# Maintenance screen

## Intent

Gather what needs attention and what has been done to the machines (US-OPE-12, US-OPE-19).

## Requirements

- Registering a job is the action of the heading.
- A strip counts the machines to attend: at or near their oil change, with what is left or overdue, and out of service. It opens the full list.
- The calendar goes back to the first record, marks jobs and damage, and never offers a future day.
- A day can be filtered by machine and a job can be registered on it. Touching a day shows it beside the calendar on wide screens and in a sheet on a phone.
- A record of a retired machine stays on the calendar and the charts, marked as retired, without a link to a history.
- The charts cover the last six months, add up each currency on its own and rank the five machines with most spend.
- Every filter lives in the address and replaces the history entry, so going back leaves the screen.
