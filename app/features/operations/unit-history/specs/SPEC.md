# Machine history

## Intent

Show everything that has happened to one machine before sending it to the workshop again (US-OPE-14, US-OPE-19).

## Requirements

- The header shows state, category, reading, hits and the next oil change.
- Jobs are grouped by month and shown ten per page; the job count and the spend per currency cover every job.
- A page past the last one shows the last page.
- Damage reports show cause, text and hits; pending ones can be completed.
- From here a job or a damage report can be started, and the photos opened when the category keeps them.
- A malformed or unknown id, or a retired machine, shows that it was not found.
