# Equipment

## Intent

One place for damage, state photos and maintenance, reached from the board.

## Requirements

- The board shows «Equipo» next to its title only in operations mode, with the number of reports to complete; reports of decommissioned machines are not counted.
- Three cards open damage, photos and maintenance, each with what is waiting.
- Two charts below: damage by cause in the last six months, and maintenance spend per month in one currency at a time.
- Each bar opens the matching filtered screen.
- Nothing of the board is removed.
