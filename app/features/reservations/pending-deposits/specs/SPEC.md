# Pending deposits## IntentLet the person in Reservas see every deposit that is still unresolved, with its amount and the reservation it belongs to, so money that belongs to a customer never hangs in the air.## In scope- Read-only list at `PATHS.RESERVATIONS.DEPOSITS`, rendered on the server, one row per deposit with status `held`, oldest first.- Each row: customer name, reservation code, the amount in its own currency and the currency name.- Empty state, error state and loading state with Spanish copy.## Out of scope- Resolving a deposit, filters, pagination, totals of any kind, a date per row, a retry button.- Route-level area gating: not part of this story. Row Level Security already returns no deposit rows to a worker without the Reservas area.- The application shell and its `<main>` landmark.## Requirements### User story (US-RES-33)Como la persona de reservas,necesito consultar qué depósitos siguen pendientes de resolver,con la finalidad de no dejar en el aire plata que es de un cliente.Un depósito pendiente es dinero de otra persona que la empresa tiene en la mano. Mientras no se resuelva sigue apareciendo en esta lista, con su monto y con la reserva a la que pertenece.### Acceptance criteria (US-RES-33)- Es plata de otra persona y no puede quedar en el aire.### Non-functional requirements- RNF-035: Los montos en dólares y en colones se muestran separados y nunca se suman en un solo total.- RNF-038: Operaciones no ve información de dinero de ninguna reserva: ni cobros, ni devoluciones, ni depósitos. Sí consulta el catálogo de precios, que es otra cosa.## Edge cases & errors- A failed read shows the error state and says an empty list must not be assumed; it is never rendered as an empty list.- Zero held deposits shows the empty state instead of the list.- USD and CRC deposits sit in the same list, each printed in its own currency; no figure combines them.## Technical notes- Reuse existing components / constants / store patterns: `Title`, `Section`, `MaterialIcon`, `UnorderedList` (`PLAIN` variant), `formatMoney`, `fetchPendingDeposits`.- Skills that apply: component-architecture, component-standards, constants-standards, styles-standards, structure-standards.## Acceptance criteria- [ ] Every deposit with status `held` appears with its amount and its reservation code.- [ ] A deposit stays in the list until it is resolved.- [ ] Each amount is shown in its own currency and no total or sum is shown.- [ ] A worker without the Reservas area receives no deposit data.- [ ] A failed read is announced as an error and never looks like an empty list.
